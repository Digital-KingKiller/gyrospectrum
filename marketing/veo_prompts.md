# 🎥 Veo 3 Video Prompts for GyroSpectrum

These prompts are designed for Google's Veo 3 video generation model to create a high-end, cinematic promotional video for GyroSpectrum.

## 🎬 Scene 1: The Problem (Chaos)
**Concept:** Visualizing the overwhelming data marketing teams face.
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic, photorealistic 8k close-up. A stressed creative director's eye reflecting endless scrolling streams of social media analytics, red charts plummeting, and chaotic spreadsheet data. Dark, moody lighting with neon red and blue accents. Cyberpunk aesthetic. The camera slowly zooms into the pupil, transitioning into a digital void. High contrast, film grain.

## 🎬 Scene 2: The Solution (The Brain)
**Concept:** Introducing the GyroSpectrum AI Core.
**Prompt:**
> Abstract 3D animation of a glowing, crystalline artificial brain pulsing with golden light in a void of deep minimalist black. Data streams (binary code and floating social media icons) are being sucked into the brain and organized into clean, structured geometric lines. Ethereal, futuristic, clean Apple-like aesthetic. Soft white lighting, glass textures, 4k resolution, ultra-detailed.

## 🎬 Scene 3: Competitor Analysis (The Scan)
**Concept:** Visualizing the AI scanning the market.
**Prompt:**
> A holographic 3D map of a digital city representing the market. A golden scanner beam sweeps across the city, highlighting specific buildings (competitors) in green. As the scanner passes, detailed analytics panels pop up in AR (Augmented Reality) floating in the air. Tech interface, Iron Man HUD style but cleaner. Motion graphics, smooth animation, high frame rate.

## 🎬 Scene 4: Content Generation (Creation)
**Concept:** AI generating perfect content.
**Prompt:**
> A macro shot of a sleek, futuristic glass keyboard. Invisible fingers type rapidly. As keys are pressed, sparks of creativity (colorful particles) fly up and form into perfect Instagram posts, Tweets, and LinkedIn articles floating in the air. The posts are glowing and vibrant. Shallow depth of field, bokeh effect, warm lighting, magical realism.

## 🎬 Scene 5: Sales Automation (The Connection)
**Concept:** Automated deals being closed.
**Prompt:**
> Network visualization. Thousands of nodes (dots) connected by thin lines. The lines turn from grey to gold rapidly as connections are made. The camera flies through this network at high speed. At the end of the tunnel, a "DEAL CLOSED" notification appears in a sleek, minimalist UI card. Fast-paced, dynamic motion blur, sense of speed and efficiency.

## 🎬 Scene 6: The Future (Conclusion)
**Concept:** The calm after the storm. Success.
**Prompt:**
> Wide shot of a modern, minimalist office with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking a futuristic clean city at sunrise. A diverse team exists but is relaxed, looking at a single floating holographic dashboard showing all green upward trends. The text "GyroSpectrum" slowly fades in with a modern sans-serif font. Hopeful, bright, clean, high-end commercial style.

---

## 🎵 Suggested Audio
- **Music:** Driving, rhythmic synthesizer track. Starts tense/chaotic, transitions to a "drop" at Scene 2 into a smooth, driving, optimistic beat.
- **Voiceover:** Deep, calm, authoritative AI voice.
